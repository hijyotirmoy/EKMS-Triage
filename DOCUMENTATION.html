EKMS Triage — System Architecture & Workflow

A comprehensive operational and clinical guide on call evaluation, AI inference cascade, geospatial facility routing, and outcome generation.

1. Executive Overview

EKMS Triage is an automated clinical navigation system built for helpline operators managing healthcare inquiries from insured workers and their dependents. It provides real-time algorithmic decision support, symptom probing, danger sign detection, and geospatial facility dispatch.

Core Operating Philosophy: Non-Doctor Assistance
Call operators are non-physicians. The system strictly analyzes reported symptoms to establish clinical urgency and route the call to the right destination. It does not fabricate disease diagnoses (e.g., Kidney Stone, Appendicitis) and never prescribes medications unless explicitly stated by the caller.
2. Tools and AI Models Used

The platform combines a multi-provider AI cascade with deterministic clinical rules to guarantee high accuracy, sub-second responses, and 100% operational uptime. Zero secret credentials, private keys, or internal endpoints are exposed.

4. Anatomy of the Triage Outcome Page (What is Displayed & Why)

When the operator completes caller probing and clicks "Generate Triage Outcome", the application presents a structured, auditable clinical result card. Below is the exact visual sequence from the top summary line to the recommended facility, along with the operational rationale for each element.

URGENT PRIORITY (ACUITY 6/10)
Urgent medical evaluation recommended within 2 to 4 hours.
Primary Summary Headline
1. Severity Badge & One-Line Summary Headline
Displays the color-coded triage acuity level (RED: Emergency, AMBER: Urgent, YELLOW: Routine, GREEN: Self-Care) alongside a clear, concise headline in plain language.
Reason Behind This: Helpline operators need instant 1-second situational awareness. During a call, the operator cannot read paragraphs of clinical notes; the top banner immediately tells them whether an ambulance is needed or if outpatient clinic referral is sufficient.
Action Timeframe:
Visit Hospital Casualty / OPD within 2–4 Hours
2. Action Directive & Time-to-Treatment Target
Provides a concrete operational directive (e.g., "Call 108 Emergency Ambulance Immediately", "Seek Casualty / OPD within 2 to 4 Hours", or "Schedule Dispensary Visit in 24 to 48 Hours").
Reason Behind This: Eliminates subjective hesitation or delays by translating clinical scores into an unambiguous, non-negotiable time window for the patient and operator.
⚠️ DANGER SIGNS DETECTED:
• Severe progressive pain
• High fever with chills
• Inability to keep liquids down
3. Red Flags & Danger Signs Box
An alert box highlighting any critical symptoms detected during call probing (e.g., radiating chest pain, breathing difficulty, sudden confusion, high fever with altered vitals).
Reason Behind This: Ensures life-threatening danger signs are never missed, gives clinical justification for high-urgency routing, and protects patient safety in medico-legal audits.

4. Caller Primary Condition / Symptom Presentation
Reports the symptom presentation strictly using descriptive clinical terminology grounded in what the caller actually reported (e.g., "Abdominal Pain" or "Persistent Cough with Fever").
Reason Behind This (Non-Doctor Protocol): Tele-triage operators are clinical navigators, not diagnosing physicians. Mentioning diseases like "Kidney Stone" or "Appendicitis" before lab tests creates false alarms, legal liability, and misguides hospital intake.

5. Recommended Health Facility (Primary & Secondary Referral)
Details the exact destination healthcare center with its designation (ESIC Hospital Casualty, ESIS Dispensary, or Empanelled Tie-Up Hospital), precise distance in kilometers, operating hours, and full address.
Reason Behind This (15 KM Rule): Direct government ESIC/ESIS centers are prioritized whenever located within 15 km. Private empanelled tie-up hospitals are only unlocked when direct government centers are farther than 15 km, optimizing resource utilization and preventing unnecessary out-of-pocket costs.
Dispatch Rationale:
Evaluated as Urgent based on symptom intensity and duration. ESIC Hospital (7.4 km) is within the 15 km radius and provides full casualty coverage.
6. Clinical Reasoning & Dispatch Rationale
A transparent breakdown explaining why this acuity tier was assigned, why this specific facility was chosen over others, and what transport mode is suitable (Ambulance vs Private Vehicle).
Reason Behind This: Equips the helpline operator with complete clinical context and empathetic talking points to clearly explain the care plan to an anxious caller or family member.
Immediate Patient Instructions:
• Rest in a comfortable position
• Keep hydrated with small sips
• Avoid solid food until doctor review
7. First-Aid & Supportive Care Instructions
Actionable non-pharmacological supportive advice that the operator reads to the caller for stabilization while traveling or awaiting assistance.
Reason Behind This: Keeps the caller calm and safe without dispensing unprescribed medications or contraindicated home remedies.

Key Rules Summary:

  • Government Priority: If an ESIC Hospital or ESIS Dispensary is within 15 km of the caller's location, the system will never assign a private facility.
  • After-Hours Coverage: On weekends and after 3:00 PM (when dispensaries are closed), patients within 15 km are directed to the 24x7 ESIC Hospital casualty, backed by 104 Tele-Doctor phone triage.
  • Remote Workers Protection: Tie-Up hospitals act as a critical safety net exclusively for workers located in distant industrial hubs where government facilities are beyond 15 km.

6. Continuous AI Learning & Feedback Portal

The platform features an active human-in-the-loop learning system that allows operational supervisors and medical officers to fine-tune AI decisions without technical downtime.
